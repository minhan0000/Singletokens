Questions

Why do I need OpenRouter?

OpenRouter gives one account access to hundreds of AI models. You add credit there, and SingleTokens uses your connection to send your messages. That way SingleTokens never handles your money.

What happens to my chats?

They're saved in your account so you can come back to them. When you send a message, it goes to OpenRouter and to the company behind the model you picked. The privacy page explains the details.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account, chats, GPTs and OpenRouter connection right away.

Is there a subscription?

No. SingleTokens is free, and OpenRouter charges per message from the credit you added. There's no monthly fee.
